SCHEDULE OF FEES - INSTITUTIONAL CLIENTS

Effective 1 July 2026. This schedule replaces all earlier schedules from that date.
All amounts are in EUR and exclude VAT.

1. Settlement

Settlement instructions are charged per instruction, by the number of instructions
settled in the calendar month. Each band is charged at its own rate:

    first 500 instructions                         EUR 1.95 each
    next 1,500 instructions (501 to 2,000)         EUR 1.45 each
    next 8,000 instructions (2,001 to 10,000)      EUR 1.00 each
    above 10,000 instructions                      EUR 0.60 each

2. Safekeeping

Safekeeping is charged monthly on the value of assets under custody at month end.
The rate of the band the total falls into applies to the whole amount:

    up to EUR 250 million                          1.50 basis points per annum
    above EUR 250 million and up to EUR 1 billion  1.00 basis point per annum
    above EUR 1 billion                            0.60 basis points per annum

3. Foreign exchange

Spot foreign exchange is charged on the EUR equivalent traded in the calendar month,
each band at its own rate:

    first EUR 10 million                           1.80 basis points
    next EUR 40 million (up to EUR 50 million)     1.20 basis points
    above EUR 50 million                           0.70 basis points

4. Corporate actions

EUR 27.50 per corporate action event processed.

5. Messaging and reporting

EUR 0.40 per message. For clients sending more than 50,000 messages a month a reduced
rate may be agreed with the relationship manager.
